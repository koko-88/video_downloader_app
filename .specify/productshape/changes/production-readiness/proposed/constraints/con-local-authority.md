## Constraint

Documented: no hidden remote uploads, paid/cloud transcription, access-control bypass or unapproved tool/model installation. No Git publishing or deployment without human authorization. Optional ASR needs a verified installed backend/model.

## Rationale

Documented: the constitution protects local data, privacy and human ownership of spending, access and release decisions.

## Consequences

Unavailable runtime/model/review/installer evidence remains explicitly blocked or not run. Candidate product definitions remain drafts until separate human approval; implementing already admitted VD-02 does not approve this recovered overlay.
